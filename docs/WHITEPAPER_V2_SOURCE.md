# 01 / SYSTEM
## One connected House
> A token, an art collection, working tools and an indie game in production.

BULLENCIAGA connects the Token-2022 $BULLEN asset with collectible art, numbered House Objects, public verification tools and community services. BULLENSAGA is the game being built within that wider world. Each part has its own purpose, rules and record.

### Live now, in production, and planned

| Status | Systems |
| --- | --- |
| Live | $BULLEN, the HERD, Signet and Cufflinks claims, automatic House Key awards and the private Salon. |
| Live | Wallet Passport, Living Ledger, market and supply tools, The Drop, referrals and community services. |
| Open during development | BULLENSAGA preorders, Founding Records, one-time contributions and monthly support. |
| In production / future scope | The game, physical rewards and later product phases. Funding milestones are not release dates. |

### The accounting rule

- An escrow deposit is a **commitment to burn**. It has not yet reduced total token supply.
- Only a confirmed Token-2022 burn is recorded as **destroyed supply**.
- A preorder, support payment or manual award is recorded for what it actually does. It cannot stand in for an on-chain burn.
- Collectible inventory, payment allocations, access rights and token supply are separate records. Public views connect them without counting the same event twice.

This edition updates the August 29 document to the systems live on September 9, 2026. Fixed issue limits and published token allocations are retained. Changing balances, prices, availability and completed transactions belong in the linked live records.

# 03 / SUPPLY REDUCTION
## Commitments become proof
> Supply falls when a burn is confirmed, regardless of which activity funded it.

| Mechanism | Published amount | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Volume schedule | 250,000,000 $BULLEN maximum | All five fixed milestones execute. |
| HERD public claims | 250,000 $BULLEN each | Deposit to escrow, followed by a confirmed burn. |
| Signet / Cufflinks public claims | 100,000 $BULLEN each | Deposit to escrow, followed by a confirmed burn. |
| Game preorders / direct support | Published payment allocation | Buybacks, burns or the published preorder fulfilment route; only actual confirmed burns reduce supply. |

### The escrow lifecycle

1. A public claimant signs a Token-2022 TransferChecked for the exact published amount into the canonical burn escrow token account.
2. The claim system verifies the token mint, source, destination, amount and transaction before fulfilment.
3. The deposit is recorded as committed. A later scheduled burn destroys the tokens.
4. The confirmed burn signature becomes the supply-reduction evidence. Commitment records are not added again as another burn.

### Maximums are conditional

A complete 1,000-piece HERD public run can commit 250,000,000 $BULLEN. All 200 first-pair House Objects entering through public claims can commit a further 20,000,000. Together with the 250,000,000 volume allocation, these three defined mechanisms have a **520,000,000-token theoretical ceiling**.

That ceiling is not a forecast or a guaranteed minimum. Preorder and manual object awards consume the same numbered stock without creating a 100,000-token deposit. A HERD claim funded through a preorder giveaway remains part of the HERD route, not an extra claim outside its cap. Other confirmed discretionary burns or payment-funded buybacks are tracked by their own actual records.

# 09 / PARTICIPATION
## The Drop and referrals
> Published eligibility, actual fee funding and records that can be checked.

### The Drop

The Drop is a **2.5 SOL** draw funded by a share of actual creator fees from qualifying buys. Its progress follows funding, not a countdown. Each completed **0.25 SOL** of qualifying House-pool buys earns an entry; small buys accumulate. Pool fees count toward buy size, but network, router and wallet fees do not.

Sales consume the newest purchase batches first. Touching a completed batch removes its entry; touching unfinished progress discards it. Later buys create fresh entries rather than restoring broken batches. Sales do not subtract fees already in the pot.

Before a draw, a public finalized eligibility snapshot is recorded ahead of the selection seed. The draw page explains the qualification rules, funding state and verification path. Buying a token does not guarantee a prize, and the funding bar does not promise when the next draw will happen.

[The Drop: rules and current draw](https://bullenciaga.com/thedrop)

### Referral records

A wallet signs a permanent, forward-only referral binding. Only eligible buys after registration enter the calculation. Weekly SOL referral rewards depend on actual qualifying activity and the published fee schedule.

Self-referral can earn the published trading-fee reward. It does **not** turn a person's own HERD mints into qualifying referred mints for the custom-art reward.

Three qualifying referred public HERD mints earn the referrer a custom 1-of-1 under the published programme. Public mint qualification is separate from trading-fee eligibility; the same word "referral" must not blur those two rules.

[Referral registration, rules and payouts](https://bullenciaga.com/refer)

### Giveaways

Published giveaways have their own eligibility and closing rules. Giveaway activity, referral qualification and Drop entries are not interchangeable balances. The records show the relevant event and outcome for each programme.

A price estimate is not a redemption value. A pending payment is not revenue received. A mint is not automatically a burn. A lock release is not destroyed supply. Each view should be read according to the event it actually records.
