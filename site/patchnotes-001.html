What changed

Shipped
01 / MOBILE BUYING

A prepared trade now opens where a mobile wallet can actually use it.

Live on the site

The same BUY $BULLEN button now understands the difference between a desktop browser and a phone. Desktop keeps the fast Jupiter path. Mobile hands the trade into the wallet that can sign it.

  • Phantom opens a native swap with $BULLEN already selected.
  • Solflare opens the prepared Jupiter trade inside its own browser.
  • Visitors already inside a wallet can keep using the embedded swap.
  • Pump.fun, Jupiter Web and copy-contract routes remain available.
02 / ONE HOUSE

The public surfaces now speak the same visual language.

Navigation, spacing, buttons, mobile safe areas and focus behaviour were brought into one House system. Stats, Tape, Chart and Curve read as connected rooms rather than unrelated tools.

  • The mobile BUY panel remains fully visible as browser chrome moves.
  • Keyboard focus stays accessible without looking like a wallet endorsement.
  • Burn proof now sits beside the Curve record instead of living on a duplicate page.
Public polish
03 / THE TAPE

Market activity is easier to read without turning wallets into content.

The Tape follows completed pool trades, preserves its history through upstream interruptions and keeps wallet identities private. Large buys, live flow and the path toward the next burn are visible without manufactured narrative.

Live market record
04 / HOUSE DESK

Approval and publication are no longer confused.

Approving a draft does not create a raid. The desk waits until the edited post or reply actually appears on X, then gives Telegram the real link. Buried cards can be recovered, and scanning can be paused when the desk should stay open without spending API credits.

Private operations
05 / TWO BOTS

BULLENBOT and SAGABOT can be inspected and recovered separately.

Each bot now has its own live state, delivery checks and controlled recovery actions. Mint and preorder media reach the correct announcement lane. A redundant Telegram edit is treated as “already current,” while a real failure shows the action required.

Private operations
06 / CONTROL ROOM

The House can be managed from an iPhone without shrinking the job.

A private, Face ID-protected Control Room now brings together orders, payments, NFT distributions, custom mints, referral obligations, House Desk controls, both bots and on-the-go post preparation.

  • Owed and paid obligations are separated instead of flattened into one total.
  • Failed or delayed events have explicit recovery controls.
  • Health messages say whether to act, inspect or do nothing.
Private operations
07 / BULLENSAGA

The story-facing flows received the same operational discipline.

Preorders can recover verified Solana payments safely, USDC instructions are clearer, fulfillment avoids duplicates, and related mint events carry the right artwork into the right bot. The public story stays clean because the machinery underneath is less fragile.

Flow hardened
08 / RESERVE CUSTODY

The volume-burn reserve is public again—and split by purpose.

Verified on-chain

All 237,500,000 reserve tokens now sit in two non-cancellable Jupiter Lock escrows. The entire balance of each lock remains unavailable until its hard cliff; neither lock drips tokens over time.

  • 25,000,000 $BULLEN is isolated for the next volume-tier burn and locked for seven days.
  • 212,500,000 $BULLEN is held in the longer reserve lock for thirty days.
  • Both escrow token accounts are included in the live circulating-supply calculation.
  • A permanent burn-only vault is the next design step; until then, each public relock remains verifiable.
The operating principle
We cannot force real activity—and we will not fake it. We can keep making the real thing easier to enter, verify and operate.

That is the work: fewer dead ends, fewer ambiguous alarms, fewer moments where someone has to understand the machinery before they can use the House.
