    Full counting rules & verification

    What counts

    Only verified qualifying swaps against the same official BULLEN / SOL pool used by the previous competition count. Normal direct buys and aggregator buys of BULLEN remain eligible; only the official pool’s BULLEN output counts. Under the 10 October 2026 amendment, a buy is excluded if that BULLEN is used to buy another BULLEN-paired token in the same verified route. The entire buy contributes neither progress nor entry credit, including any leftover BULLEN. This exclusion applies from the campaign’s original opening, not only to new trades.

    Entries are the smaller of (a) qualifying buys minus sells since opening, and (b) the increase in the wallet’s BULLEN balance since opening. Negative results count as zero. The aggregate is rounded down to whole BULLEN: 1 whole BULLEN = 1 entry. No cap.

    Transfers do not create purchase credit. Selling old holdings also reduces net buying and the balance increase. Tokens must remain in the same wallet through the closing snapshot. The House custodian and liquidity pools cannot win.

    Example: buy 100,000, sell 20,000 and retain an 80,000 increase over the opening balance → 80,000 entries. Transfer out another 10,000 → at most 70,000 entries.

    When it closes

    The shared target counts cumulative verified qualifying official-pool buys under the amended rules, including eligible purchases by wallets excluded from prizes. Sells never reverse progress. It closes at the first finalized balance snapshot whose fully reconciled history reaches 250M; all qualifying transactions through that snapshot count.

    The scored closing snapshot and a future finalized Solana block seed must be published before selection. Five weighted selections without replacement assign #777, #703, #159, #31 and #959 in that order. At least five eligible wallets are needed to award all five. Incomplete or ambiguous chain data pauses selection.

    Prizes are delivered manually. The previous competition’s completed results remain below.
