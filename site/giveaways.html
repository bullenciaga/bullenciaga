The final count

The campaign ran from 26 September 2026 at 05:20:13 UTC to the first fully reconciled closing snapshot above the 250M qualifying-buy target. Final entries were sealed at slot 455,416,504.

Entries were the smaller of qualifying buys minus sells and the increase in a wallet’s BULLEN balance since opening, rounded down to whole BULLEN. Existing holdings, gifts and transfers in created no purchase credit.

The 10 October amendment applied to the entire campaign. BULLEN purchases used to acquire another BULLEN-paired token were excluded, including separate purchases proven to have fully funded a paired-token buy. The final count excludes 97 acquisitions totalling 5,893,087.977274 BULLEN.
