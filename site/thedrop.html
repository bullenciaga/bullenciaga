How it works

  • buying is entering. nothing to connect, nothing to sign, nothing to register.
  • one entry per 0.25 SOL bought. no cap. entries stack.
  • only buys count. a sell earns nothing and fills nothing.
  • the bar is fees, not volume. how much buying it takes depends on what those buys pay, and that rate moves.
  • hold until it fires. sell before the draw and this round's entries go with it.
  • the dev wallet and the pool are excluded, by address.
  • the snapshot is published before the seed exists. then the result. every time.
