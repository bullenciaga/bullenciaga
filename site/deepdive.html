READ THE FOOTNOTES. THEY MATTER.

What the numbers mean.

A BULLEN-specific LTH proxy

Our 7, 14, 30 and 60-day views estimate how long the remaining balance has stayed in its token account. They are not Bitcoin’s 155-day, entity-adjusted LTH metric.

We work backwards through finalized transactions, reconcile pre/post balances and assign the remaining tokens to the most recent net inflows. This is a first-in, first-out (FIFO) model: older units are treated as leaving first.

One denominator at a time

By default, the percentage uses eligible wallet holdings. Known program-controlled accounts, the main liquidity pool, the mint burn escrow, reserve accounts and the public dev wallet are separated. You can also view aged holdings as a share of circulating supply.

“Wallet” means an address. We do not infer common ownership or label unfamiliar wallets as independent people.

Limits, clearly stated

Transfers to a different token account reset the age there, including transfers between someone’s own wallets. Activity inside a single transaction is netted. Aged balances can include gifts and transfers, not just purchases.

Unreconciled histories remain unknown and stay in the denominator. No balance is assumed old because data is missing. Frozen accounts and custody arrangements do not prove voluntary conviction.

Snapshots, not predictions

Holder data is checked approximately every minute against finalized Solana balances. Changed accounts are reverified before publication. Market feeds refresh every minute while this page is visible. An interrupted update keeps the previous snapshot with its original date.

Historical comparisons begin with the first collected snapshot; we do not manufacture an earlier trend.
