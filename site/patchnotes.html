05 / DEEP DIVE

Look beyond
the latest candle.

How much supply has stayed put? How concentrated are eligible wallet holdings? Where are the tokens now? Deep Dive brings holding-age bands, supply distribution, concentration and market activity into one page.

Its long-term held supply estimate lets you choose a 7, 14, 30 or 60-day window. Freshness indicators and methodology explain the figures, including the accounts excluded from the wallet comparison.

It measures the estimated age of balances still held. That gives past holding behaviour more context; it does not predict who will sell next.

06 / WHAT IF I HELD?

Did the extra moves
earn their keep?

Paste any public Solana wallet address to compare its available $BULLEN trading history with a holding scenario. No wallet connection or signature is needed.

The comparison uses the same funding and end point. It counts gains and losses on sold tokens and tokens still held, and gives successful exits and buybacks credit. Holding can win. Trading can win. The result should tell you which happened.

Returns are measured in SOL, with the history window and any estimates shown. Received tokens may start at their market value when received, so the result is not a lifetime return based on the owner's original purchase price. A dated group study sits alongside the live wallet lookup.

07 / THE DEVELOPER RECORD & BURN RESERVE

On the record.
Under lock.

The Developer Record puts the reviewed account's buys, sells and burn activity into a dated, inspectable ledger. Its 28 September snapshot links the underlying transactions and distinguishes personal contributions from burns carried out through House mechanisms.

The burn reserve has also been renewed. On 2 October, the remaining 212.5M $BULLEN was relocked for 60 days, with a new cliff on 1 December. The next 25M tranche keeps its separate weekly renewal schedule while its volume milestone is pending.

Two locks keep the next scheduled burn separate from the remaining reserve. Each tranche still needs its milestone and its lock to open before it can be burned. The lock page carries the new escrow, dates and on-chain links.
