01 / LIVING LEDGER

One timeline.
Sources attached.

The Living Ledger brings market summaries, supply changes, custody receipts, collectible records and verified deliveries into one public chronology. Entries retain their source or receipt so the record can be followed beyond the headline.

House Record explains what we ship. Living Ledger records what happens. Neither guesses who controls a wallet or why it moved.

02 / WALLET PASSPORT

What an address holds.
Nothing it has to sign.

A single read-only view brings together $BULLEN, The Herd, House Objects and BULLENSAGA Founding Records held by a Solana address. Paste an address, or connect a wallet to supply its public address.

No signature or spending permission is requested. Burned assets are excluded from the holdings view, and collectible artwork is shown in full, with its label below.

07 / HOUSE DESK & CONTROL ROOM

A sharper inbox.
A more useful phone.

House Desk has been rebuilt around selected accounts and fresh, standalone posts. Replies, quotes and reposts are excluded; freshness is adjustable, with a five-minute default. Review budgets remain bounded, and publishing still requires human approval.

The installed stream-capable rebuild passed local checks. Live-stream access and end-to-end delivery remain to be verified when monitoring is enabled; this is not a claim of measured seconds-fast delivery.

The iPhone Control Room adds reserve proof and deadline monitoring, broader public-page checks and clearer House Desk intake status. Historical giveaways no longer create false unfinished-delivery alerts merely because a winner later moved a prize or the original draw label predates delivery.

Private apps updated
08 / COMMUNITY BOT

Keep the right card
within reach.

BULLENBOT’s raid resurfacing now recognizes its own buy notifications as messages in the same group. Existing burial thresholds and cooldowns still apply; it does not repost the raid after every buy.

Admins can also set or reset an individual post’s like target without changing the actual measured engagement, other engagement goals or the campaign timer.

The changes passed automated checks and production smoke tests. No synthetic public buys or raids were used for verification.
