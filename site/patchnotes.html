01 / DISCORD

Conversation has
a new address.

The BULLENCIAGA Discord brings conversation, collecting, art and BULLENSAGA together. Start in the salon, show a piece in the herd, share work in the atelier or find the game rooms. Choose the interests and optional alerts you want to follow.

You do not need crypto or an NFT to join the public House. A quick human check opens participation; collectible verification adds the roles and holder rooms you qualify for.

02 / WALLET VERIFICATION

From Discord
into your wallet.

Private verification replies now offer direct Phantom and Solflare buttons. On mobile, the invitation opens in the wallet’s browser with its verification session intact. Inside the wallet, one VERIFY button continues the process.

You review a sign-in message naming your Discord account. Current collectible holdings determine your roles. Verification requests no transaction, approval or transfer; you can unlink your wallet later.

Phantom & Solflare
03 / TAKE PART

Show up.
Play together.

/daily signs you into the guest book and earns House points. Build a streak, claim a weekly bonus and use /rewards for cosmetic badges. Seven visited days earns HOUSE REGULAR; thirty earns HOUSE FIXTURE.

/quiz starts a three-round HERD team challenge. /flex shares full artwork in the conversation, using either a piece number or a named custom. /rank shows your own progress.

House points are cosmetic and separate from the shared X raid score. Sharing a piece does not claim ownership.

Inside Discord
04 / CONNECTED BOTS

Two places.
One shared campaign.

Staff can start and manage the BULLENCIAGA raid campaign from Discord or Telegram. Both communities follow the same campaign and weekly X score. BULLENSAGA’s Telegram campaign remains separate.

Discord also receives automatic buy, collectible-sale and listing cards, mint records, official X posts, preorder records and supply updates. Optional notification roles let members choose their pings; routine card updates stay quiet.

BULLENBOT and SAGABOT have reached 2.0 on Telegram, with expanded command directories and grouped public links. Use /bullen with BULLENBOT and /saga with SAGABOT. Market shortcuts, wallet lookups and named artwork are easier to find across the two platforms.

05 / HOUSE KEY & INNER ROOMS

A pair.
A Key. A room.

An eligible wallet holding a Signet and Cufflinks can earn a House Key. Their serial numbers do not need to match. One wallet can earn one Key, and each source piece can support one award.

Current Key holders can enter the website’s Inner Rooms and the Discord inner salon after verification. The website salon now supports image sharing with an optional caption, a preview before posting and larger views of images in the conversation.

06 / BULLENSAGA EDITIONS

Choose the game.
Choose your edition.

The preorder page now separates GAME EDITIONS from GAME EDITIONS + COLLECTIBLES. Game-only Standard and House editions offer card checkout without a wallet.

The collectible editions retain their own inclusions and checkout paths. The choice is visible before checkout, so players can see what belongs to the game and what comes with a collectible edition.

07 / COLLECTION BROWSING

More of your collection,
in one search.

Gallery wallet searches now include House Objects and BULLENSAGA Founding Records alongside the numbered HERD collection. The existing filters still apply, and clearing the wallet search returns to the original gallery.

The House Key has a lighter gallery preview, and collectible artwork keeps its full frame. Wallet Passport remains available for a separate read-only view of an address’s holdings.

08 / COMMUNITY FINISHES

Recognizable
in the small things.

The BULL server tag, custom role icons, expression packs and selected role gradients give the Discord its own identity. SERVER BOOSTER gets a holographic style; collectible and community roles have their own colors.

  • Wallet buttons use wallet logos. Role and action buttons use icons matched to their purpose.
  • Command and guide cards share a consistent layout, with their actions grouped inside.
  • Flex and quiz cards keep readable proportions, including long trait names and mobile screens.
  • Mobile verification preserves its private invitation when opening the wallet app.

These changes have passed automated checks and production verification, with native wallet and Discord checks where noted in the release records.
