01 / LEDGER COMPATIBILITY

A hardware wallet
has a place here.

Using a Ledger account through a compatible wallet? The new Using Ledger? controls explain the setup and let you enable compatibility mode. Connect through your existing wallet, unlock the device and open its Solana app.

Referrals, Inner Rooms and House Object ownership checks can use an offline signed proof. That proof is never submitted to the network and does not charge a fee. Actual mints, trades and transfers retain their normal costs.

Ledger compatibility is new and needs more testing across wallets and devices. If anything feels awkward or fails to connect or sign, let us know which wallet and device you use and what happened. Your feedback will help us polish the flow.

02 / A CLEARER CONNECTION

Help where
you need it.

Ledger compatibility is set once inside the wallet picker and remembered in this browser. The picker stays available even when only one wallet is detected. Expanded help and wallet lists fit smaller screens.

Wallet Passport now uses the shared wallet chooser. House Object claims handle interrupted submissions more carefully, so an existing pending claim can be resumed. The buy page also explains Ledger setup for its Jupiter swap, which retains its own wallet connection and approvals.

02 / Raid standings

Showing up leaves a record.

Live
03 / THE RAIDERS

The names behind
the noise.

/raiders brings the recorded raid scores into a public page: the all-time top five and a top twenty for every recorded week. Look up a handle in the weekly standings and follow the current week as points arrive.

The page refreshes automatically, and a new current-week card appears each Monday at 00:00 UTC. Collection fixes improve reply, quote and repost coverage while preventing the same activity from being awarded twice.

These are recorded points, not a complete reconstruction of every historical interaction. Older collection gaps remain disclosed, and a catching-up notice identifies delayed or incomplete collection.

04 / GIVEAWAY VERIFICATION

A counter with
something behind it.

The buy counter has been recovered and reconciled against finalized transactions. More swap-fee patterns and newer transaction formats are handled correctly, addressing the cases that interrupted verification.

A visible last-verified time and catching-up state distinguish a current total from a retained one. Private monitoring flags stalled verification. Campaign rules and existing entries stay intact.

05 / COMMUNITY SAFEGUARDS

A quieter bot.
A closer watch.

Telegram impersonation checks now revisit known members between messages, helping catch accounts that rename themselves after joining. These are periodic checks of the known roster, not instant detection of every profile change.

Daily supply posts are protected against duplicate delivery. Routine bot upgrade notices are shorter, keeping maintenance from crowding the conversation.

06 / COLLECTION & MOBILE

Small improvements.
Every time you visit.

Gallery cards and piece labels are more consistent. Mobile swipes settle more smoothly, and rapid taps or swipes keep up as you move between pieces. Shortlist and close controls have cleaner sizing and alignment.

Tablet navigation follows the same compact layout as mobile. Artwork loading and image-studio recovery have been improved, and wallet dialogs remain usable with expanded guidance on smaller screens.
