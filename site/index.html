FAQ
what is $BULLEN?

a Solana Token-2022 asset at the centre of a connected House: the 1,000-piece Herd, numbered House Objects, and BULLENSAGA. The burn schedule and every completed burn remain verifiable on-chain.

can the team mint more tokens or freeze wallets?

check the Mint Authority and Freeze Authority badges near the top of this page — they're read directly from the chain, live, every time this page loads.

how do the burns actually work?

there are three transparent paths. volume milestones release and destroy the locked 237.5M burn reserve; Herd claims commit 250,000 $BULLEN each to public burn escrow; public House Object claims commit 100,000 each to that escrow. escrow deposits are labelled committed, not destroyed, until a scheduled Token-2022 burn reduces supply on-chain. preorder and manual awards consume numbered inventory but do not fabricate a token burn.

why does the site say “committed” instead of “burned”?

a claim is shown as committed as soon as its $BULLEN reaches the public burn escrow. circulating supply changes only when the separate Token-2022 burn instruction lands on-chain. the registry shows the commitment immediately; the supply pages wait for the completed burn.

what are House Objects and how are they claimed?

The Signet and The Cufflinks begin the collection with 100 numbered editions of each. verified holders enter first for 48 hours; a public claim requires exactly 100,000 $BULLEN and commits it to the public burn escrow. a wallet below that balance cannot reserve a number or create a transaction. preorders and manual awards use the same numbered inventory without inventing a burn.

what is BULLENSAGA, and how does it connect to BULLENCIAGA?

BULLENSAGA is a separate game and lore universe built around Count, Record and Measure. its founding records connect back to the House through the Promise, the Triad and shared numbered House Object inventory, but the two worlds keep distinct characters, rules and visual identities.

what is the dev wallet used for?

it holds the remaining scheduled dev allocation and also pays for listings, dex updates, promotions, trending and other services. the public burn escrow is a separate account. every payment and completed burn is independently checkable on Solscan.

where can i verify any of this myself?

open “the receipts” beneath the roadmap. the dev-wallet and burn buttons lead to their separate Solscan accounts; the House Object registry links to the shared ledger, and the whitepaper explains how those records reconcile.

why has my trade not appeared on The Tape yet?

The Tape follows the primary $BULLEN / SOL pool through public upstream data that is normalized and cached before display. indexing can lag, so a confirmed swap may take time to appear. your wallet and the Solscan transaction remain the source of truth while the public feed catches up.

how do i actually buy it?

see the "how to buy" section above — wallet, SOL, copy the contract address, swap. four steps, easy to follow.

how do i mint one of the herd nfts?

there is no sol price. connect your wallet and commit 250,000 $BULLEN to the public burn escrow to claim a random piece from the collection — you pay only the solana network fee. the supply changes when the scheduled Token-2022 burn lands on-chain.

can i see the whole collection before minting?

yes — scroll up to browse all 1,000 pieces, search by name or number, and see what's already been claimed.

how do i check which pieces i own?

connect your wallet in the gallery above and switch to "my collection" — it shows exactly what you hold, live from the chain.

are some pieces rarer than others?

yes. beyond the standard trait rarity, ten pieces — five true 1-of-1s and five numbered legendaries — also carry a share of ongoing creator fee revenue. full breakdown is in the whitepaper.

is there more detail on how all of this works?

the full whitepaper covers the token, all three burn paths, The Herd, House Objects, BULLENSAGA founding records, collection authority, and the fee-sharing model in one place.
