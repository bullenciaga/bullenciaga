Why two locks

Ready to burn. Not free to move.

The next scheduled tier destroys 25M $BULLEN. That tranche and the remaining 212.5M have independent lock schedules; each stays locked until its own cliff.

  1. If the $1M cumulative-volume tier arrives before the cliff, the 25M tranche can be claimed after its cliff and burned in one curated transaction.
  2. The 25M tranche stays on a weekly renewal cycle while its tier is pending; it was relocked on 6 October, with its current cliff on 11 October. The remaining 212.5M was relocked on 2 October for 60 days, with a new cliff on 1 December. Later burns wait for both their milestone and the relevant lock to open.
  3. Every relock creates a new escrow account. The House publishes it here and updates circulating-supply accounting against the exact Token-2022 account.
The permanent design

A burn-only vault comes next.

The present Jupiter locks are an interim custody layer, not the final automation. The intended replacement is a reviewed, program-controlled vault that can destroy an exact tier tranche but cannot withdraw, transfer, cancel or redirect it. Until that system is ready, the House will keep using visible hard cliffs and publishing each new escrow.
