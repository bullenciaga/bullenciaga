Keep the pieces
that catch your eye.

Edition 004 brought more of your collection into one gallery. Now you can save pieces to a shortlist and compare their artwork, traits, gallery rarity and available marketplace listings side by side.

02 / THE IMAGE STUDIO

Make it yours.

Arrange up to nine pieces into an X banner, phone background or print. Choose a layout and background, reorder the artwork, add a title, and decide whether to show piece names and the House signature. Download the result as a PNG.

Original artwork keeps its full composition. On mobile, the preview stays visible while the options scroll beneath it. Adding a piece keeps the last finished preview on screen while the next one loads, with recovery for slow or incomplete image requests.

03 / THE THIRD DOOR

An unfinished entry.
A few questions.

The Third Door is a short interactive dialogue set in the Estate: a way to meet its characters, feel the atmosphere and get a little closer to the lore. It is a web story, not the actual gameplay or a playable demo of the final game. It offers a taste of the kind of conversation being developed for BULLENSAGA.

Choose one of six investigators and speak with the Steward. Follow the questions through the Workshop and the Flooded Cloister, check what you find, and return to your notes before choosing a conclusion. Different investigators bring different lines to the conversation.

Letters appear as you read; a tap reveals the rest. Questions and places are marked once asked or checked, five observations collect in your folio, and conclusions can be reconsidered. Pixel-art settings, character portraits, quiet selection clicks and music complete the scene. Music starts automatically where the browser permits, or after your first interaction, and respects your saved choice.

04 / SUPPORT DEVELOPMENT

Help build
what comes next.

The new funding page brings preorders and direct support into a public view of development funding. Its cumulative $75,000 campaign sets out milestones for a playable demo, a workstation, time to build, game completion and a full voice cast.

Give once or support monthly through the available checkout options. The page explains contributor rewards, lifetime support progress and the 10% $BULLEN buyback-and-burn allocation on direct and monthly contributions. Funding progress reflects recorded net receipts; reaching a milestone does not mean the work or reward has already been delivered.

Preorder editions keep their stated benefits. Estate Edition includes a short in-game dedication; the contributor figurine has its own eligibility and production terms.

See it taking shape.

Public work-in-progress updates remain available. New arrivals can choose VERIFY COLLECTIBLES or JUST EXPLORING, with the latter keeping the public House wallet-free.

07 / THE BURN RESERVE

Dates, balances
and receipts.

The reserve page now shows current estimated dollar values alongside $BULLEN balances. The 25 million $BULLEN lock was renewed on 9 September, with a 16 September cliff; the separate 212.5 million reserve retains its 2 October cliff.

Locked-supply displays follow the verified cliff dates, and previous custody receipts stay in the Living Ledger. Burn tracking also handles delayed reads more carefully, retaining its last reliable record when a read is incomplete.

10 / MOBILE & GALLERY

More room
for the artwork.

Gallery cards now use uniform save buttons and footer heights, including House Objects and Founding Records. Repeated serial numbers have moved out of those card titles; the number remains in the piece details.

Lightboxes and the Vault sit above the mobile header, so the artwork and close controls can be reached. Square artwork keeps its proportions. The image studio groups its controls more cleanly, with space around dropdown chevrons and between explanatory text and actions.

11 / ACROSS BOTH SITES

The small things
should stay quiet.

Mobile layout and font-loading fixes reduce shifts as pages open or browser toolbars move. The persistent navigation is present from the first page render, with the approved House typography retained.

Keyboard focus indicators now appear when navigating with the keyboard. Tapping, typing or opening a panel no longer leaves an unwanted highlight behind. Close icons, the BULLENSAGA back arrow and media controls have also been aligned and sized consistently.

12 / COMMUNITY FEEDS

Keep the conversation
in view.

Discord buy cards have a more compact layout. Active raid cards can resurface when conversation buries them, without repeating the role ping, and the redundant timed raid reminder has been removed.

These are refinements to the connected community announced in edition 003. The public rooms, collectible verification and existing campaign records remain the foundation.
